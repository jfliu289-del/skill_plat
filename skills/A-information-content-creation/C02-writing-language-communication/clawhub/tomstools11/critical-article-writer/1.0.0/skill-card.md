## Description: <br>
Generate draft articles, outlines, and editorial content matching a distinctive analytical, skeptical voice with sharp critical commentary, conversational tone, and strategic humor. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Writers, strategists, and editorial teams use this skill to draft critical technology articles, outlines, threads, quick takes, and research-backed opinion pieces in a skeptical but fair voice. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Drafts may include inaccurate, incomplete, or unsupported factual claims about companies, products, markets, or people. <br>
Mitigation: Review drafts for factual accuracy, cite credible sources for major claims, and verify links, statistics, and quotations before publishing. <br>
Risk: Critical commentary can create reputational or legal risk if the tone is unfair, claims are not substantiated, or criticism targets identifiable entities. <br>
Mitigation: Review for fairness, sourcing, defamation risk, privacy concerns, and appropriate internal or legal approval before public release. <br>
Risk: The skill is designed to match a distinctive voice, which may raise authorship or permission concerns when used on behalf of a named person. <br>
Mitigation: Use the voice-matching behavior only with appropriate permission and review final drafts with the intended author or accountable publisher. <br>
