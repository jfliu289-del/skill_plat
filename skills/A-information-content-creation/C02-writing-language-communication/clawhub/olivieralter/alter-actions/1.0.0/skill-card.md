## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Selected text or files in Alter may be processed by Alter actions, including AI or web-enabled actions. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid selecting secrets, credentials, or confidential material before triggering actions. <br>
Risk: The reviewed artifact references an index.js helper that is not included in the artifact. <br>
Mitigation: Review any separately obtained helper file before running command-line examples. <br>
Risk: The skill is intended for Alter workflows on macOS. <br>
Mitigation: Install only when you use and trust the Alter macOS app. <br>
