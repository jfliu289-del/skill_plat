## Description: <br>
Remove signs of AI-generated writing from text and rewrite it to sound more natural while preserving the original meaning and tone. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jazzyong2023](https://clawhub.ai/user/jazzyong2023) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Writers, editors, and developers use loadpage to review selected text or files for AI-writing patterns and produce a more natural rewrite with an optional change summary. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can rewrite documents and may unintentionally change legal, compliance, evidentiary, or policy-sensitive text. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only on selected text or files, review diffs before applying edits, and avoid sensitive documents unless those changes are explicitly intended. <br>
Risk: A rewrite may alter nuance, tone, or meaning beyond what the user intended. <br>
Mitigation: Review the rewritten text before use and compare it against the original for meaning preservation. <br>
