## Use Case: <br>
Developers, designers, and content teams use this skill to guide agents through fal.ai-powered text-to-image generation, image remixing, and targeted image editing workflows. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Prompts, input images, private URLs, and generated image metadata may contain sensitive information and are processed by fal.ai services. <br>
Mitigation: Do not submit confidential, regulated, or sensitive personal data unless approved, and strip image metadata before sharing generated outputs externally. <br>
Risk: The skill requires a fal.ai API key and may incur usage costs or encounter rate limits. <br>
Mitigation: Keep FAL_KEY out of source control, monitor fal.ai dashboard usage and pricing, and choose lower-cost models when high-resolution or premium generation is unnecessary. <br>
