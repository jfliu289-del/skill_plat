## Description: <br>
Scamper guides agents through the SCAMPER creative thinking method to develop, adapt, and expand ideas for brainstorming, frameworks, hackathon concepts, and blockers. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[enzoricciulli](https://clawhub.ai/user/enzoricciulli) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to apply SCAMPER lenses to ideas, products, frameworks, and blockers so an agent can produce structured creative options and strongest-angle recommendations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated ideas may be appended to notes/ideas.md when the user accepts the disclosed save workflow. <br>
Mitigation: Only approve saving when the ideas should be persisted, and review the generated markdown before relying on it. <br>
