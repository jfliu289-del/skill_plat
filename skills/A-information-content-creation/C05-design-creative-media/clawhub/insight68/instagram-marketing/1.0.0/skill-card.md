## Description: <br>
Generate Instagram marketing content from product URLs, including product details, image or video suggestions, captions, calls to action, and hashtag strategy. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[insight68](https://clawhub.ai/user/insight68) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External marketers, creators, and commerce operators use this skill to turn public e-commerce product URLs into ready-to-adapt Instagram feed, carousel, story, reel, caption, hashtag, and posting-strategy packages. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Fetching user-provided URLs can expose private, internal, admin, cart, or token-bearing pages from the user's environment. <br>
Mitigation: Use only public product pages that are appropriate to fetch, and avoid URLs containing credentials, session tokens, private paths, or internal hosts. <br>
Risk: Generated marketing copy may contain inaccurate product claims or platform, advertising, or compliance issues. <br>
Mitigation: Review product details, claims, captions, hashtags, and calls to action before posting or scheduling content. <br>
