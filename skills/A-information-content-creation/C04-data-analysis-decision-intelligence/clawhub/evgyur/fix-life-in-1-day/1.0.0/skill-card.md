## Description: <br>
Guides users through 10 structured self-reflection and life-design sessions based on Dan Koe's viral article. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[evgyur](https://clawhub.ai/user/evgyur) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
ClawHub users use this skill for guided self-reflection, journaling, and personal planning across 10 psychology-inspired sessions. It is not therapy or crisis support. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill asks for sensitive journal-like self-reflections and saves answers in the local workspace. <br>
Mitigation: Review or delete saved session files, insights, reminders, and exported documents if those reflections should not be retained. <br>
Risk: The skill uses a forceful self-reflection style and could be mistaken for therapy or crisis support. <br>
Mitigation: Use it only as self-guided journaling and planning support; seek qualified professional or emergency help for clinical, crisis, or safety needs. <br>
