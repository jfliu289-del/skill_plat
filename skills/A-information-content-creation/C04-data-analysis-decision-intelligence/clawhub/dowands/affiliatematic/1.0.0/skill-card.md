## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill instructs users to load JavaScript from affiliatematic.com on their websites, which requires trusting that third-party service with script execution in the page context. <br>
Mitigation: Install only if you trust affiliatematic.com, test on staging first, and avoid private or regulated pages until you understand how page content is processed. <br>
Risk: The artifact includes performance and revenue claims that may not match a specific site's audience, content, or affiliate program behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the claims as marketing estimates unless independently verified with your own analytics and affiliate reporting. <br>
