## Description: <br>
Benchmarks competitor keywords, content, backlinks, AI citations, and traffic share into strengths, weaknesses, and an action plan. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, SEO, and sales enablement teams use this skill to compare competitors, label metric confidence, and produce a prioritized competitor brief or battlecard. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Competitor strategy, user site metrics, and research summaries may be retained in agent memory when the skill saves durable findings. <br>
Mitigation: Use an ephemeral workspace for confidential work, or redact and remove sensitive details before allowing memory writes. <br>
Risk: Competitor metrics can be incomplete, estimated, stale, or tool-dependent. <br>
Mitigation: Require each metric to be labeled as Measured, User-provided, Estimated, or N/A, and avoid treating estimates as measured facts. <br>
