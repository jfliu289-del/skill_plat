## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Product, marketing, and performance data may be sent to BlogBurst during content generation, publishing, attribution, or account-specific operator workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only when that data sharing is acceptable for the product and organization, and prefer public demo mode when account-specific data is not needed. <br>
Risk: The optional BLOGBURST_API_KEY could expose account access if pasted, printed, logged, or placed directly in command examples. <br>
Mitigation: Configure BLOGBURST_API_KEY only through the OpenClaw environment or secret store, and never reveal it in chat, logs, shell output, or visible examples. <br>
Risk: Prepared or published content could affect public channels, founder reputation, or domain trust. <br>
Mitigation: Keep human review in place before public posts go live, and use BlogBurst for Reddit or Hacker News drafts only rather than automated promotion. <br>
Risk: Marketing claims can become misleading if product facts, customer stories, metrics, or growth outcomes are fabricated or overstated. <br>
Mitigation: Ground content in available buyer evidence and product facts, ask for missing facts, and avoid guaranteed growth claims. <br>
