## Description: <br>
Plan and design garden layouts with companion planting, spacing, sun requirements, crop rotation, and exportable layout management for small gardens and farms. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[johstracke](https://clawhub.ai/user/johstracke) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Home gardeners and small growers use this skill to plan garden beds, compare companion planting and spacing guidance, track seasonal rotations, and export layouts for reference. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Garden layout names and exported plans may contain personal or sensitive details. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sensitive details in bed or plant names, and export only to paths you intend to create or overwrite. <br>
