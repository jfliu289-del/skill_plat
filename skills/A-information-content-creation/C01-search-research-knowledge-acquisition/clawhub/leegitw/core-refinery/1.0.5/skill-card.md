## Description: <br>
Find the core that runs through everything - the ideas that survive across all your sources. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
Employees, external users, developers, and knowledge workers use this skill to synthesize three or more source materials and identify recurring principles, invariant ideas, and Golden Master candidates. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Source material and synthesized outputs may include confidential or proprietary information. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide source material that is appropriate for the configured model provider, and review outputs before sharing them. <br>
Risk: Synthesized invariants and Golden Master candidates may repeat source mistakes or present agreement as stronger evidence than it is. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as pattern evidence for human review, not as verified truth or canonical guidance without validation. <br>
