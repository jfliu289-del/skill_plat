## Description: <br>
Discover what two sources agree on — find the signal in the noise. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[leegitw](https://clawhub.ai/user/leegitw) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and analysts use this skill to compare two related sources and identify shared patterns, unique principles, and meaningful divergences without treating agreement as proof of truth. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Comparison results may present agreement between two sources as stronger evidence than it is. <br>
Mitigation: Treat shared patterns as validation signals rather than proof, and use judgment before relying on conclusions. <br>
Risk: Source material is processed by the user's configured agent model, which may be cloud hosted. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide source material that is appropriate for processing by the configured agent model and trust boundary. <br>
