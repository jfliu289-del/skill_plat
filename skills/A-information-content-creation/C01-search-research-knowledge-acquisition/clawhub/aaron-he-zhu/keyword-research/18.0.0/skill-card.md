## Description: <br>
Discovers, scores, and clusters keywords for SEO and GEO planning, prioritizing search volume, difficulty, intent, and topic clusters from provided or connected data. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External marketing, SEO, and content strategy users use this skill to research keyword opportunities for a page, topic, campaign, market, or language. It produces prioritized keyword briefs with intent labels, volume and difficulty evidence, GEO opportunities, topic clusters, content calendar guidance, and next steps. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may use connected SEO or Search Console data for keyword research. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid connecting sensitive marketing, launch, or client data unless the agent is authorized to use it for the research task. <br>
Risk: The skill may save research summaries and keyword priorities into host memory files. <br>
Mitigation: For sensitive work, instruct the agent not to persist results or manually review generated memory entries after the run. <br>
