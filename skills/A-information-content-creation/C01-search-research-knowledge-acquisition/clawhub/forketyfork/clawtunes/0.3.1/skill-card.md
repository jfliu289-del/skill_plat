## Description: <br>
Control Apple Music on macOS via the `clawtunes` CLI for playback, search, volume, shuffle, repeat, AirPlay, catalog lookup, and playlist management. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill when they want an assistant to control Apple Music on a Mac, including playback, volume, search, AirPlay routing, and playlist tasks through the clawtunes CLI. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can let an assistant control Apple Music on a Mac, including playback, volume, AirPlay output, and playlist changes. <br>
Mitigation: Install only when this behavior is desired, and clarify ambiguous music requests before allowing the assistant to control Apple Music. <br>
