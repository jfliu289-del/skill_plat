## Description: <br>
AI Daily Briefing helps an agent produce a morning overview of overdue tasks, today's priorities, calendar items, and recent meeting context. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jeffjhunter](https://clawhub.ai/user/jeffjhunter) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Employees and productivity-focused users use this skill to ask an agent for a concise daily briefing that combines to-do items, calendar information, and recent meeting context into one prioritized plan. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Briefings may surface sensitive task, meeting, memory, preference, or calendar details from local productivity sources. <br>
Mitigation: Install and invoke the skill only where the agent is allowed to read those sources, use explicit briefing prompts in shared contexts, and keep sensitive notes out of referenced folders when they should not appear. <br>
