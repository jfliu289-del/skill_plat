## Use Case: <br>
External users and developers use this skill to verify factual answers with current public web sources before responding. It is intended for concise source-backed answers and for surfacing uncertainty when sources conflict or cannot be found. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Factual questions or rewritten search queries may be sent to external search or fetch providers. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid using the skill with secrets, confidential business information, personal data, or regulated data. <br>
