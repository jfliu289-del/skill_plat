## Description: <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External developers, operators, and agents use this skill to guide GoHighLevel account setup, marketplace app creation, and OAuth credential preparation for integrations. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: GoHighLevel client secrets and OAuth tokens can grant access to CRM, contact, calendar, agency, or sub-account data. <br>
Mitigation: Use only the required OAuth scopes, confirm the account being connected, and keep secrets and tokens out of chat, screenshots, logs, and repositories. <br>
Risk: The skill includes a referral sign-up URL that may not be appropriate for every user. <br>
Mitigation: Use a direct GoHighLevel sign-up URL when the user does not want to use the referral link. <br>
