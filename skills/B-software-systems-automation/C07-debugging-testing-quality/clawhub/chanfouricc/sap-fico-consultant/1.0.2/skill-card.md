## Use Case: <br>
SAP finance and controlling practitioners, consultants, and implementation teams use this skill to answer FI/CO configuration, troubleshooting, integration, closing, and S/4HANA migration questions in French technical SAP terminology. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad SAP keyword activation can trigger the skill in conversations where SAP FI/CO support was not intended. <br>
Mitigation: Review activation settings and disable automatic activation or narrow keywords when deploying into mixed-topic assistants. <br>
Risk: SAP configuration, migration, accounting, and OSS/KBA guidance may affect production financial systems. <br>
Mitigation: Validate recommendations against official SAP documentation and authorized SAP staff before applying changes, especially in production. <br>
Risk: The skill is designed to answer in French technical SAP terminology, which may not suit all users or review workflows. <br>
Mitigation: Set expectations for French-format answers or adapt the prompt before deployment when another response language is required. <br>
