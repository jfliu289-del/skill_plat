## Use Case: <br>
External users can use this skill for in-the-moment anxiety support, including breathing exercises, grounding techniques, thought reframing, and anxiety logging. It is self-help guidance and not a substitute for professional mental-health care. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat anxiety self-help guidance as a replacement for medical or mental-health care. <br>
Mitigation: State that the skill provides self-help guidance only and direct users to professional care or crisis resources when appropriate. <br>
Risk: Anxiety logs may contain sensitive health information. <br>
Mitigation: Keep logs local and treat them as private health information. <br>
Risk: Crisis situations or self-harm thoughts require immediate support beyond this skill. <br>
Mitigation: Use the listed crisis resources, such as 988 or Crisis Text Line, or seek professional help immediately. <br>
