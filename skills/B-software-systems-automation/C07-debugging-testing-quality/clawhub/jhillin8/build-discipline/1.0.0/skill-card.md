## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill as a local discipline coach to define daily commitments, track streaks, stack habits, and review patterns across sleep, exercise, nutrition, focus, and recovery. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may store personal routine or health-adjacent details such as sleep, exercise, nutrition, focus, and recovery commitments. <br>
Mitigation: Track only details the user is comfortable storing locally, and review or delete commitments and streak history through the local OpenClaw environment when needed. <br>
Risk: Broad activation phrases may trigger discipline coaching in loosely related conversations. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the user wants to create, update, or review discipline commitments before changing any tracked habit state. <br>
Risk: Habit and accountability guidance could be treated as authoritative health or performance advice. <br>
Mitigation: Keep suggestions lightweight and user-directed, adjust commitments conservatively, and avoid presenting habit guidance as medical, fitness, or nutrition advice. <br>
