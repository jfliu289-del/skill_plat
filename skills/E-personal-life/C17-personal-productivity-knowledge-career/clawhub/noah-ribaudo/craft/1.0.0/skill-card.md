## Use Case: <br>
Craft users and developers use this skill to search, read, create, update, organize, and manage Craft documents, blocks, tasks, daily notes, and collections from an agent-assisted CLI workflow. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill can create, update, complete, or delete Craft content. <br>
Mitigation: Preview the target document, block, or task before changing it, and confirm exact IDs before deletion. <br>
Risk: The referenced Craft CLI install script was not included in the scanned bundle. <br>
Mitigation: Verify that the referenced script is present and trustworthy before installing or running it. <br>
