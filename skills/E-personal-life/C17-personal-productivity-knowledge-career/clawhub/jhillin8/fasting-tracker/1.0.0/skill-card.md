## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to start and stop fasting sessions, check elapsed fasting time, review fasting history, configure fasting protocols, and receive milestone reminders. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat fasting milestones or extended-fast guidance as medical advice. <br>
Mitigation: Present fasting information as tracking support, encourage healthcare consultation for extended fasts or medical conditions, and avoid substituting skill output for clinical advice. <br>
Risk: A fasting session may be started, ended, or tracked incorrectly if the user command is ambiguous. <br>
Mitigation: Use explicit user commands and confirm before starting or ending a fast when possible. <br>
Risk: Local fasting logs, schedules, reminders, and background tracking may expose sensitive personal routine data on the user's device. <br>
Mitigation: Install only when local storage of fasting data is acceptable and keep logs on trusted devices. <br>
