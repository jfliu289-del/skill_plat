## Description: <br>
Manage sourdough starters with feeding schedules, hydration calculations, health tracking, and baking preparation. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[akhmittra](https://clawhub.ai/user/akhmittra) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External home bakers, beginner sourdough users, and experienced bakers use this skill to plan feedings, calculate hydration and feeding ratios, troubleshoot starter health, and prepare active starter for baking timelines. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Starter health and contamination guidance may be incorrect or insufficient for a user's specific food-safety situation. <br>
Mitigation: Review suggested food-safety and mold guidance, discard starters when contamination is suspected, and do not rely on the skill as a substitute for user judgment. <br>
