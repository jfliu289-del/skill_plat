## Description: <br>
Crucial Conversations Coach helps users prepare for, navigate, and debrief high-stakes conversations using the Tools for Talking When Stakes Are High methodology. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[pors](https://clawhub.ai/user/pors) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and employees use this skill to prepare scripts, emails, and debriefs for high-stakes or conflict-prone conversations while preserving mutual purpose and respect. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may share sensitive workplace or personal conversation details. <br>
Mitigation: Treat conversation details as sensitive and avoid retaining or exposing unnecessary personal or confidential information. <br>
Risk: Users may over-rely on coaching output for legal, medical, psychiatric, crisis, or formal HR decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for communication preparation only and consult qualified professionals for those domains. <br>
Risk: Suggested phrasing may be unsuitable if facts, relationship dynamics, or organizational context are incomplete. <br>
Mitigation: Review and adapt drafts for accuracy, respect, and context before using them. <br>
