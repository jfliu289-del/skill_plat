## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and crypto researchers use Crypto Cog to request CellCog-supported crypto research across tokens, DeFi protocols, on-chain activity, portfolios, whitepapers, and smart contract risk. Outputs should be treated as research support, not financial, legal, or tax advice. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Crypto prompts can expose sensitive financial data or wallet details. <br>
Mitigation: Do not share seed phrases, private keys, wallet recovery data, exchange credentials, exact account identifiers, or unnecessary exact balances; use ranges or anonymized holdings where possible. <br>
Risk: The skill relies on a CELLCOG_API_KEY for CellCog access. <br>
Mitigation: Store CELLCOG_API_KEY in a secure environment variable or secret manager and avoid committing it to files or prompts. <br>
Risk: Crypto research outputs may be mistaken for financial, legal, or tax advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as research support and verify conclusions with primary sources or qualified professionals before making decisions. <br>
