## Description: <br>
Gekko is an AI-powered DeFi portfolio manager for the Base network that helps analyze yield opportunities, manage portfolio allocations, and provide market intelligence using vault APY data from Morpho and Yearn. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users, developers, and DeFi analysts use this skill to query Gekko's remote A2A service for Base-network yield analysis, vault allocation recommendations, token analysis, market intelligence, and DeFi chat responses. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: User prompts and portfolio-related inputs are sent to Gekko's remote service. <br>
Mitigation: Do not enter seed phrases, private keys, wallet credentials, API keys, confidential trading plans, or nonpublic strategy details. <br>
Risk: Yield, allocation, and trading recommendations may be incorrect, stale, or unsuitable for a user's risk tolerance. <br>
Mitigation: Verify recommendations, market data, APY figures, and smart-contract risks before moving funds. <br>
