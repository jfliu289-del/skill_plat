Mitigation: Review claims, calculations, assumptions, and sources before relying on the plan for business decisions or sharing it with investors or partners. <br>
Risk: Business plans can include confidential company, customer, or financial details. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid providing sensitive information unless it is appropriate to include it in the assistant context. <br>
