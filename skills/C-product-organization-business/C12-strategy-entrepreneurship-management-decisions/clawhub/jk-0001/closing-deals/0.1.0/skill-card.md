## Description: <br>
Close sales deals consistently as a solopreneur by using decision-stage psychology, closing techniques, stall recovery, contract-to-payment flow, and post-close relationship setup. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External solopreneurs and sales-focused operators use this skill when a prospect is at the decision stage, when a deal is stalling, or when they need practical scripts for moving from proposal to signed contract and payment. It supports repeatable, trust-based closing conversations without adding software, credentials, or automation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Sales scripts may introduce untrue urgency, overstated ROI claims, or unsuitable contract, invoice, and payment language if reused without review. <br>
Mitigation: Review and adapt any contract, invoice, payment deadline, ROI claim, or urgency language before sending it, and keep all sales conversations truthful and legitimate. <br>
