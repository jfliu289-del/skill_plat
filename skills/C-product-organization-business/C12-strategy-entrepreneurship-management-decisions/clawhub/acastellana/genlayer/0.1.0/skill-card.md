## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Conceptual blockchain examples may be mistaken for production-ready implementation guidance. <br>
Mitigation: Treat LLM, web-fetch, staking, escrow, dispute-resolution, and treasury examples as conceptual and add privacy controls, validated data sources, bounded model outputs, approval flows, timelocks or circuit breakers, and independent smart-contract security review before production use. <br>
Risk: Protocol explanations may become outdated as GenLayer evolves. <br>
Mitigation: Check current GenLayer documentation and project references before relying on the skill for investment, governance, or implementation decisions. <br>
