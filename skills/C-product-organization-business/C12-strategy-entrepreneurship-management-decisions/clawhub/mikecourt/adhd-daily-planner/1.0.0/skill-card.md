## Description: <br>
Time-blind friendly planning, executive function support, and daily structure for ADHD brains. Specializes in realistic time estimation, dopamine-aware task design, and building systems that actually work for neurodivergent minds. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Use Case: <br>
External users and employees can use this skill to ask an agent for ADHD-friendly daily and weekly planning support, including time estimation, task prioritization, transition buffers, dopamine-aware task design, and executive function checklists. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may over-rely on ADHD, medication, or crisis-planning guidance as medical or emergency support. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill as informational planning support only; use medication notes for tracking or prescriber questions, and seek local emergency or crisis support for self-harm risk, danger, or acute mental-health distress. <br>
Risk: Planning templates may produce unrealistic schedules or unsuitable advice for an individual user's situation. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated plans before relying on them, keep commitments small and adjustable, and adapt recommendations to the user's current capacity and professional guidance. <br>
