## Description: <br>
Uncover the real "job" customers hire your product to do. Goes beyond features to understand functional, emotional, and social motivations. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[artyomx33](https://clawhub.ai/user/artyomx33) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Product teams, founders, marketers, and customer researchers use this skill to analyze why customers hire a product, identify functional, emotional, and social jobs, compare alternatives, and prioritize underserved needs. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill may be invoked for broad product or customer-motivation questions. <br>
Mitigation: Confirm the product, user segment, and analysis scope before producing recommendations. <br>
Risk: Customer interviews, product strategy, or business research shared with the assistant may be confidential. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering sensitive customer or business data unless the assistant environment is approved for that information. <br>
