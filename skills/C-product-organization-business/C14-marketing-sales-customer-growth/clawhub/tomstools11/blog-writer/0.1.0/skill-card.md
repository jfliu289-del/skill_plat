## Description: <br>
This skill writes blog posts, articles, and long-form content in a distinctive direct, conversational, opinionated style, using provided research and examples to support drafting, revision, Notion publication, and finalized example retention. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[tomstools11](https://clawhub.ai/user/tomstools11) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Writers, marketers, founders, and operators use this skill to draft and refine thought leadership or blog content in Tom Panos's voice, especially on AI, productivity, sales, marketing, and technology topics. The skill supports research review, voice matching, iterative feedback, Notion publication, and saving final posts as future style examples. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Draft content may be published to a configured Notion database as part of the skill workflow. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill only when publication to that Notion workspace is intended, and review drafts before allowing publication. <br>
Risk: Sensitive notes, confidential research, or unpublished proprietary material could be included in drafts or retained as examples. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid supplying sensitive materials unless the Notion publication and finalized-example retention behavior is acceptable. <br>
