## Description: <br>
Provides ADHD body-doubling focus sessions with micro-step protocols, frequent check-ins, dopamine resets, and session history tracking. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jankutschera](https://clawhub.ai/user/jankutschera) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to run ADHD body-doubling sessions that help them start tasks, maintain accountability, recover from blockers, and reflect on session outcomes. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Local session history may retain task descriptions, energy levels, blockers, and outcomes. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid entering confidential work, health, financial, or personal details unless you are comfortable retaining them locally, and periodically review or delete the history directory. <br>
