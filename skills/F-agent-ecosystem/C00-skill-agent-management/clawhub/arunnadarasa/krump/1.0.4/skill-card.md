## Description: <br>
A dance skill designed to teach OpenClaw agents the fundamentals of Krump, including its history, fam system, music, crews, events, and other related topics. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill as a Krump dance reference for explaining fundamentals, composing text-based choreography, describing character rounds, and applying tournament scoring guidance. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Energetic dance movements may create ordinary physical safety risks if users try them without preparation. <br>
Mitigation: Warm up, use clear space and suitable footwear, start slowly, adapt or skip difficult moves, and stop if pain or dizziness occurs. <br>
Risk: The skill states that its knowledge base extends only up to 2017, so community, event, or cultural details may be outdated. <br>
Mitigation: Treat historical and community details as an educational starting point and verify current facts before relying on them. <br>
Risk: Tournament guidance mentions public voting or comments. <br>
Mitigation: Do not allow an agent to post public votes or comments unless the user explicitly approves that action. <br>
