## Description: <br>
Help answer questions about Catholicism accurately and respectfully (Catechism-first). Provides a structured response format, common topic map with CCC references, and short prayer/reference snippets. Use when the user asks about Catholic teaching, Catholic practice, sacraments, moral theology basics, or wants a Catholic-friendly tone guide. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Religious guidance may be mistaken for authoritative pastoral, legal, or medical advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat the skill as a reference and style aid; verify important doctrinal or personal matters with primary Catholic sources or qualified clergy, and seek emergency services for immediate danger. <br>
Risk: The CCC topic map is a lightweight pointer map and may omit nuance or relevant primary sources. <br>
Mitigation: Use the provided references as starting points and confirm important answers against the Catechism, Scripture, councils, encyclicals, or qualified pastoral support as appropriate. <br>
Risk: Local helper scripts produce simple keyword-based lookup output. <br>
Mitigation: Review script output before relying on it, and open the underlying reference files for the full context. <br>
