## Description: <br>
Record, export, and share your AI coding sessions as replayable buildlogs. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[espetey](https://clawhub.ai/user/espetey) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers and engineers use this skill to record, export, annotate, upload, and share AI-assisted coding sessions as replayable buildlogs for tutorials, documentation, debugging, and learning. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Uploaded buildlogs may include source code, prompts, logs, internal paths, secrets, or file snapshots. <br>
Mitigation: Keep autoUpload off, consider private buildlogs, disable file-content snapshots for private projects, and review recordings before upload. <br>
Risk: Session recordings may be shared more broadly than intended. <br>
Mitigation: Use private sharing settings where appropriate and delete buildlogs from buildlog.ai when they should no longer be available. <br>
