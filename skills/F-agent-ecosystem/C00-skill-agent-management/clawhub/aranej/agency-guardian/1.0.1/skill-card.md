## Description: <br>
Gentle reminders to stay human while using AI. Reflection, not restriction. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aranej](https://clawhub.ai/user/aranej) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to receive occasional reflection prompts, boundary reminders, and concise quotes that encourage independent judgment while working with AI assistants. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Reflection prompts and boundary nudges may be mistaken for professional advice in health, financial, or relationship contexts. <br>
Mitigation: Keep the prompts framed as reflection support and encourage the user to rely on their own judgment or appropriate professional advice for high-stakes decisions. <br>
Risk: Occasional reminders could interrupt users who prefer direct assistance. <br>
Mitigation: Use the documented configuration option to disable boundary nudges when they are unhelpful. <br>
