## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use this skill as an educational Jungian reflection aid for shadow work, dream interpretation frameworks, archetypal analysis, active imagination, addiction/recovery reflection, individuation concepts, and psyche mapping. It is not for diagnosis, therapy, crisis support, addiction treatment, or authoritative dream interpretation. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may mistake Jungian reflection for diagnosis, therapy, crisis support, or addiction treatment. <br>
Mitigation: Use only as an educational reflection aid and seek qualified human support for diagnosis, therapy, addiction treatment, suicidality, psychosis, severe dissociation, withdrawal, overdose risk, or crisis situations. <br>
Risk: Shadow work, dream work, active imagination, and addiction/recovery reflection can feel destabilizing for some users. <br>
Mitigation: Stop exercises if distress rises, keep reflection grounded, and refer users to qualified human support when symptoms feel intense or destabilizing. <br>
