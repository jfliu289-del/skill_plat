## Use Case: <br>
Agents and users invoke this legacy LYGO champion persona when they want advisory questions, investigation plans, OSINT checklists, and source-first reasoning around possible censorship, suppression, or missing context. New installs should prefer the successor skill, lygo-champion-council. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may install this deprecated legacy persona when the maintained successor is more appropriate. <br>
Mitigation: For new installs, prefer lygo-champion-council unless compatibility with this legacy DELTA9RA persona is specifically required. <br>
Risk: The censorship and suppression framing can make speculative claims feel stronger than the evidence supports. <br>
Mitigation: Keep outputs source-grounded, separate observed facts from inferences and unknowns, and require receipts or verification plans before drawing conclusions. <br>
Risk: Information-integrity or OSINT workflows can drift into harassment, doxxing, illegal intrusion, or coercive behavior. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill as an advisor only, follow the artifact behavior contract, and limit recommendations to lawful, non-coercive collection of public or authorized evidence. <br>
