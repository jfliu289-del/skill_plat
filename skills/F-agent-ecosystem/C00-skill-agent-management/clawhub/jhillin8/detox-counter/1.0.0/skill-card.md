## Description: <br>
Track any detox with customizable counters, symptom logging, and progress milestones. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[jhillin8](https://clawhub.ai/user/jhillin8) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and wellness-focused individuals use this skill to start and monitor cleanse or detox plans, log symptoms, track milestones, and summarize progress for personal review or discussion with a healthcare provider. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users could over-rely on detox notes or symptom patterns as medical advice. <br>
Mitigation: Use the skill for wellness tracking only and seek professional care for severe, persistent, or concerning symptoms. <br>
Risk: Wellness logs may contain sensitive personal information if exported or shared. <br>
Mitigation: Keep logs local by default and export or share them only intentionally. <br>
