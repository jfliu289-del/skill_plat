## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill for daily depression self-support, including mood check-ins, small behavioral activation tasks, self-care prompts, small-win tracking, and pattern reflection. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may treat the skill as medical care during depression or crisis. <br>
Mitigation: Present it as self-help support only and direct users in crisis to emergency services or an appropriate crisis line. <br>
Risk: Broad distress phrases may activate the skill when a user needs urgent or region-specific help. <br>
Mitigation: Encourage users outside the U.S. or in immediate danger to use local emergency services or a region-appropriate crisis resource. <br>
Risk: Mood notes and pattern tracking can contain sensitive mental health information. <br>
Mitigation: Keep data local as described by the artifact and avoid adding cloud sync, analytics, or sharing without explicit review. <br>
