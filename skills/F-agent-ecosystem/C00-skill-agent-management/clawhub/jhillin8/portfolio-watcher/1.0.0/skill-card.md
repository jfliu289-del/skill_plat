## Use Case: <br>
External users use this skill to monitor stocks, cryptocurrency, and ETF holdings through conversational commands, including price checks, price alerts, and portfolio performance summaries. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may share sensitive financial details such as holdings, quantities, purchase prices, and alert targets with the agent environment. <br>
Mitigation: Share only the portfolio details needed for tracking, and do not provide brokerage logins, account numbers, credentials, or other secrets. <br>
Risk: Price checks and portfolio summaries may be stale or unsuitable as the sole basis for financial decisions. <br>
Mitigation: Verify prices independently before acting and treat the output as informational rather than financial advice. <br>
