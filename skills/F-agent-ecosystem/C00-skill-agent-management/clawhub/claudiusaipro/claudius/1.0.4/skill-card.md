## Description: <br>
Claudius provides cryptocurrency market intelligence, including prices, technical analysis, trends, risk assessments, and educational explanations through a Node.js CLI bridge. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[claudiusaipro](https://clawhub.ai/user/claudiusaipro) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT <br>


## Use Case: <br>
External users and agents use Claudius to ask cryptocurrency market questions and receive text answers about prices, indicators, sentiment, risks, and investment-related context. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Crypto questions and the user's IP address are sent to Claudius-operated servers. <br>
Mitigation: Do not include seed phrases, private keys, exchange API keys, wallet addresses tied to private activity, account details, or proprietary trading plans in prompts. <br>
Risk: Buy, sell, or risk guidance may be incomplete, outdated, or unsuitable for a user's financial situation. <br>
Mitigation: Treat responses as informational market context rather than professional financial advice, and verify important decisions with trusted independent sources. <br>
