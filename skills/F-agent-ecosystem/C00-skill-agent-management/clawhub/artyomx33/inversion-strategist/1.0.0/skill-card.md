## Description: <br>
Flip problems upside down by asking how to guarantee failure, then turning those failure paths into avoidance strategies and anti-checklists. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[artyomx33](https://clawhub.ai/user/artyomx33) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Developers, operators, and other users apply this skill during planning or review to identify likely failure paths, categorize their severity, and convert them into practical avoidance checklists. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Broad trigger phrases such as inversion or avoid failure may activate the skill during general planning discussions. <br>
Mitigation: Review the trigger language before installation and invoke the skill intentionally when inversion analysis is desired. <br>
Risk: Speculative failure paths may be mistaken for validated operational or business advice. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as decision-support prompts and verify assumptions with appropriate domain review before acting. <br>
