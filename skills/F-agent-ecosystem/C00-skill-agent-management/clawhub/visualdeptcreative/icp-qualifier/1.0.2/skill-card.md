## Description: <br>
Qualifies DTC e-commerce leads for Visual Dept with ICP criteria and a 100-point scoring rubric for outreach prioritization. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[visualdeptcreative](https://clawhub.ai/user/visualdeptcreative) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Sales and outreach teams use this skill to evaluate DTC Shopify brands against Visual Dept's ideal customer profile and decide whether a lead should be prioritized, nurtured, or skipped. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global, for qualifying United States-based DTC leads. <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Demographic, age, and United States-only qualification criteria may create business or compliance risk if used without review. <br>
Mitigation: Require human review of scoring criteria and outreach decisions before using the results in sales workflows. <br>
Risk: Lead contact details and outreach hooks may be inaccurate or subject to privacy, platform, and anti-spam requirements. <br>
Mitigation: Verify public contact information before outreach and comply with applicable privacy, platform, and anti-spam rules. <br>
