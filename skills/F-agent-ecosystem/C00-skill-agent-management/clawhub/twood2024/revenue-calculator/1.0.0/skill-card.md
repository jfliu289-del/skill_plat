## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Revenue and break-even numbers may be mistaken for financial forecasts because they depend on simple formulas and user-provided assumptions. <br>
Mitigation: Treat outputs as estimates, review formulas and assumptions before business use, and compare low, medium, and high scenarios. <br>
Risk: Pricing, cost, conversion, and churn inputs can contain sensitive business assumptions. <br>
Mitigation: Provide only the assumptions needed for the calculation and avoid unrelated confidential data. <br>
Risk: Some bundled reference and example files are placeholders. <br>
Mitigation: Rely on the active workflow, pricing reference, report template, and revenue-calc.py behavior rather than placeholder documentation. <br>
