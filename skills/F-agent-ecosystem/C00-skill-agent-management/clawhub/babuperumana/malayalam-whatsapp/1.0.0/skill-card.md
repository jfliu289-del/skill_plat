## Description: <br>
Understands Malayalam and Manglish WhatsApp messages and replies politely using culturally appropriate language in matching script style. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[babuperumana](https://clawhub.ai/user/babuperumana) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
People drafting WhatsApp replies in Malayalam or Manglish use this skill to understand messages, translate common phrases, and produce polite culturally appropriate responses in the same script style. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: WhatsApp messages may contain private or sensitive conversation content. <br>
Mitigation: Avoid sharing message content you would not want processed by the agent. <br>
Risk: Generated Malayalam or Manglish replies may miss cultural nuance or the sender's intent. <br>
Mitigation: Review replies before sending, especially for sensitive personal or business conversations. <br>
