## Description: <br>
Optimizes existing content for AI-generated answer and citation surfaces by improving structure, source attribution, factual density, quotable passages, and GEO readiness. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[aaron-he-zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/aaron-he-zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>
MIT-0 <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Marketing, SEO, and content teams use this skill to audit and transform existing pages or drafts for AI citation readiness across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Claude, and related answer engines. It produces implementation-ready content changes, scoring summaries, schema guidance, and handoff notes for review before publishing. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Generated claims, citations, schema recommendations, or publishing suggestions could be inaccurate, unsupported, or misapplied to a specific brand or page. <br>
Mitigation: Review generated claims, source citations, schema, and publishing recommendations before use; label unavailable metrics as N/A and use connected SEO or AI-monitor data only when allowed by workspace policy. <br>
Risk: The skill may suggest saving content summaries or using external SEO and AI-monitor tools in workflows where that is not appropriate. <br>
Mitigation: Only save memory summaries or connect external tools after user confirmation and when the workspace policy permits it. <br>
