## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: The skill uses a CellCog API key and may send prompts, learning materials, code, or documents to CellCog. <br>
Mitigation: Use only if the publisher and CellCog terms are acceptable; avoid private student records, confidential documents, proprietary code, and regulated data unless privacy and retention requirements are met. <br>
Risk: Tutoring, study, or coding guidance can be incorrect, incomplete, or inappropriate for a specific class, exam, or project. <br>
Mitigation: Review outputs before relying on them, verify important answers against course materials or trusted references, and adapt generated work to the applicable academic or professional requirements. <br>
