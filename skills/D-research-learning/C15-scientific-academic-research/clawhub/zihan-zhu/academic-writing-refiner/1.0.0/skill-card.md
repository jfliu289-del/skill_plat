## Description: <br>
Refines academic and research writing for computer science papers, including drafts, LaTeX sections, camera-ready revisions, and rebuttals for top-tier venues. <br>

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use. <br>

## Publisher: <br>
[Zihan-Zhu](https://clawhub.ai/user/Zihan-Zhu) <br>

### License/Terms of Use: <br>


## Use Case: <br>
Researchers, students, and technical authors use this skill to polish computer science papers, section drafts, LaTeX prose, camera-ready revisions, and reviewer rebuttals while preserving citations, math, labels, and author intent. <br>

### Deployment Geography for Use: <br>
Global <br>

## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: Users may paste unpublished papers, reviewer comments, or sensitive research text into the assistant. <br>
Mitigation: Only provide confidential or unpublished material when the user is comfortable having the assistant process that content. <br>
Risk: The skill may be asked to refine claims, results, or citations that are missing, unclear, or factually uncertain. <br>
Mitigation: Flag missing citations, unclear experimental details, and potential factual concerns instead of inventing claims or results. <br>
